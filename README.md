
# 🔎 Digital Detective — Operation Blackout

**Think like a detective. Investigate like an expert.**

An interactive cybersecurity investigation experience created for the BCS Breakout Initiative at Heriot-Watt University Dubai.

Step into the role of a cybersecurity investigator at **Aurelia Global**. A suspicious email, an unfamiliar login, and unexpected access to sensitive company files suggest that something has gone wrong.

Your mission is to investigate the evidence, identify the attack, reconstruct the incident, and deliver your final verdict.

## 🚨 The Investigation

Operation Blackout takes participants through five interactive missions:

| Mission | Investigation |
|---|---|
| 01 | Would You Trust This Email? |
| 02 | Identify the Red Flags. |
| 03 | Investigate the Email. |
| 04 | Examine the Evidence. |
| 05 | What Really Happened? |

Each mission introduces a new piece of the investigation, challenging participants to think critically and connect the evidence.


## 🏆 Investigator Ranking System

Every investigation earns **Investigation Points (IP)**. Build your score, unlock achievements, and progress through five investigator ranks.

| Rank | Required Points |
|---|---:|
| Trainee Analyst | 0 IP |
| Threat Spotter | 80 IP |
| Security Investigator | 160 IP |
| Cyber Detective | 240 IP |
| Elite Incident Responder | 320 IP |

**Maximum score: 380 IP**

### Achievements

- 🔍 **Eagle Eye** — Identify all four red flags.
- 🧩 **Header Hunter** — Correctly interpret email authentication.
- 📁 **Evidence Expert** — Inspect every evidence record.
- ⏱️ **Timeline Master** — Correctly reconstruct the incident.
- ✦ **Incident Commander** — Answer all three final verdict questions correctly.

Progress, points, evidence, and achievements are saved locally in the browser.

## 🕵️ The Challenge

The investigation follows a fictional corporate phishing incident involving:

- A lookalike sender domain.
- A suspicious verification link.
- A mismatched reply-to address.
- Credential submission.
- An unfamiliar account login.
- Unexpected access to finance files.

Participants must distinguish between confirmed evidence, reasonable conclusions, and information that remains unknown.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open the investigation website.
2. Begin Operation Blackout.
3. Complete each mission in sequence.
4. Inspect the digital evidence carefully.
5. Reconstruct the incident timeline.
6. Submit your final verdict.
7. Review your investigator report, score, rank, and achievements.
