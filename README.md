
# 🔎 DIGITAL DETECTIVE
### OPERATION BLACKOUT — CASE FILE 001

**One email. One compromised account. Can you uncover what happened?**

Welcome to *Digital Detective*, an interactive cybersecurity investigation where you step into the role of a security analyst investigating a suspected corporate phishing incident at **Aurelia Global**.

Inspect suspicious emails, uncover hidden red flags, investigate digital evidence, reconstruct the attack timeline, and deliver your final verdict.

---

## 🚨 THE MISSION

Aurelia Global has detected unusual account activity following a suspicious IT verification email.

An employee may have exposed their login credentials. An unfamiliar login has since been detected, and confidential file access is under investigation.

Your task is to determine what happened, identify the evidence, and recommend the appropriate response.

**The evidence is waiting. The investigation starts with you.**

---

## 🕵️ YOUR INVESTIGATION

Complete five interactive missions:

| Mission | Investigation |
|---|---|
| 01 | **Would You Trust This Email?** — Examine a suspicious corporate email and make your first decision. |
| 02 | **Identify the Red Flags** — Find the warning signs hidden in the message. |
| 03 | **Investigate the Email** — Examine sender details, destination links, and email authentication results. |
| 04 | **Examine the Evidence** — Analyse simulated security logs and reconstruct suspicious activity. |
| 05 | **What Really Happened?** — Deliver your verdict and recommend an incident response. |
